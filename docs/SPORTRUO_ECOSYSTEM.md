# SPORTRUO Sport Ecosystem

## Product direction
SPORTRUO is a sports-industry infrastructure and intelligence ecosystem. It is not a gambling or betting platform.

## Core services
- Talent Acquisition & Scouting
- Athlete / Player Management
- Clubs & Teams
- Academies & Grassroots Development
- League Registration
- Competition & Tournament Registration
- Stadium & Venue Registration
- Officials, Coaches, Scouts & Agents
- Transfers & Recruitment
- Sports Statistics & Data
- AI Sports Intelligence and non-gambling prediction analytics
- Events & Ticketing
- Sports Media
- Sponsorship & Partnerships
- Sports Marketplace
- Sports Community
- Verification & Identity

## Ecosystem flow
Athlete → Academy → Scout → Agent → Club → League → Competition → Stadium → Fans → Media → Sponsors → Sports Data → AI Intelligence

## Explicit exclusions
- No betting
- No wagering
- No odds
- No sportsbook
- No gambling wallet
- No gambling promotions
- No gambling affiliate functionality

## Implementation principle
Build SPORTRUO as an independent product, preserving clean boundaries so it can later integrate with the wider Xedruo ecosystem without becoming dependent on Xedruo.
